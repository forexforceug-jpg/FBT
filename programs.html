Our Programs

Comprehensive care for children and mothers in Jinja, Uganda

What We Do

Programs That Transform Lives

First Breath Trust operates four core programs designed to address the immediate needs of abandoned children while tackling the root causes of child abandonment.

Program 01

Child Care & Rehabilitation Centre

Our flagship program provides a safe haven for newborns and infants who have been abandoned or are at risk. We offer:

  • Safe sleeping spaces and nutrition support
  • Early childhood development activities
  • Access to medical services including immunizations
  • Treatment and special care for sick infants
  • Provision of clothing, hygiene, and psychosocial care
Program 02

Mother Rehabilitation & Empowerment

We believe that to save the child, we must also support the mother. Our program combines psychosocial support with hands-on vocational training across multiple trades:

Tailoring
Hairdressing
Baking
Handicrafts
Soap Making
Agriculture
Catering
Crocheting

Each mother also receives counseling, parenting training, and reintegration support.

Program 03
New

Technology & Digital Skills

In today's digital economy, technology skills open doors to better-paying work. We train young mothers and youth in practical computer and digital skills so they can earn income online or start digital businesses.

  • Basic computer literacy (Windows, typing, email, internet)
  • Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Smartphone repairs & maintenance
  • Graphic design basics (Canva, Photoshop)
  • Social media & digital marketing
  • Freelancing & online work skills (Upwork, Fiverr)
  • Mobile money & digital finance literacy
  • Web design basics (HTML & CSS)
Program 04

Community Outreach & Advocacy

We work to prevent child abandonment before it happens through community engagement:

  • Awareness campaigns on child rights and protection
  • Partnerships with local authorities, hospitals, and organizations
  • Community outreach to identify at-risk mothers before abandonment occurs
  • Referral programs for medical and social services
  • Joint programs with faith-based institutions and community leaders

What Sets Us Apart

The First Breath Difference

Focus on Newborns & Infants

Unlike many organizations that focus broadly on street children, we target newborns and infants, ensuring they receive immediate care from the first breath of life.

Mother-Centered Approach

We go beyond rescue to focus on rehabilitating and empowering mothers, thereby tackling the root causes of child abandonment.

Digital Skills for the Future

We're one of the few organizations in Jinja teaching mothers digital and technology skills — preparing them for the modern economy.
