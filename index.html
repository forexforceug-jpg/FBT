Our Mission

A Safe Haven for Every Child

We provide holistic care and rehabilitation to children born and abandoned on the streets, those from teenage mothers, children of street families, and those born to mentally ill parents.

Rescue & Shelter

Providing immediate safety, nutrition, and medical care to abandoned infants and at-risk babies.

Health & Wellness

Ensuring access to immunizations, treatment, and special care for sick infants and children.

Mother Empowerment

Rehabilitating teenage and vulnerable mothers through counseling, life skills, and vocational training.

Family Reintegration

Working to reunite children with families and communities whenever safe and possible.

Our Impact

Breaking Cycles of Neglect and Poverty

Unlike many organizations that focus broadly on street children, First Breath Trust uniquely targets newborns and infants, ensuring they receive immediate care from the first breath of life.

  • Immediate rescue and medical care for abandoned infants
  • Comprehensive rehabilitation for teenage and vulnerable mothers
  • Vocational training for long-term self-reliance
  • Family reintegration programs where safe and possible
