Why Give

Your Support Makes a Difference

Every donation to First Breath Trust directly supports our mission to provide holistic care and rehabilitation to abandoned children and vulnerable mothers in Jinja, Uganda.

$25
Provides a week of nutritious meals for a baby
$50
Covers medical check-ups and immunizations for one child
$100
Supports a mother through vocational training for a month
$250
Provides shelter and care for a child for one month

All donations go directly to supporting our programs. We are committed to transparency and accountability in all our operations.
